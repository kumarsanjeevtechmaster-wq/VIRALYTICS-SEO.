1SEO ka matlab — ek dukaan se samjhiye

Sochiye aapki ek dukaan hai. Google ek bahut badi market hai jahan log cheezein dhoondne aate hain. Aapki website ki har ek page dukaan ki ek almari hai. Jo shabdon se log khoj karte hain, unhe keyword kehte hain. Google market ka chowkidar hai — wo tay karta hai ki aapki almari kitni aage dikhegi.

Chowkidar teen cheezein dekhta hai:

1Samaan hai kya? — Page mein asli jawab likha hai, ya sirf keyword baar-baar likh diya?
2Log aapka naam lete hain? — Doosri websites aapki taraf link deti hain (backlinks) — yehi "zuban par hona" hai.
3Ghusna aasan hai? — Page tez khulta hai, har page tak raasta hai, koi page band nahi pita.
Ek aham sudhaar"Jise keyword Google mein daal diya, wo upar aa gaya" — aisa nahi hota. Keyword sirf itna tay karta hai ki aapka page kis sawaal ka jawab hai. Upar isliye aate hain kyunki wo jawab baaki 9 natijon se behtar hai. Keyword zaroori hai, kaafi nahi.
Aapki sabse badi taakatKoi bhi duniya mein "Google par number 1" ka waada nahi kar sakta. Google ka maalik Google hai. Jo aisa bole, wo jhooth bol raha hai. Aap waada nahi, report denge — yahi wajah hai ki log agency ko mahine-mahine paise dete rehte hain.

2Ye system kaise kaam karta hai — poora chakkar

Sabse pehle ye samajh lijiye ki dashboard aapke liye kya karta hai aur aap kya karte ho. Ye sabse important baat hai — isse samajh aayega ki aap machine par bharosa kyun kar sakte ho, aur kahan aapka haath lagana zaroori hai.

Ek analogi: health-checkup machine + doctor

Sochiye ye ek health-checkup machine hai aur aap doctor. Machine khud sab naap leti hai — BP, sugar, heartbeat — aur aapko ek report de deti hai jisme laal/peele flags laga hua hai ki kahan dikkat deni hai. Par machine dawai nahi likhti — dawai doctor likhta hai, patient ki manzoori se. Yahi poora dashboard hai.

Jo cheezein khud (bina aapke) hoti hain

Naapta hai: Aapki site ke pages ek-ek karke padhta hai — title, description, links, tooti cheezein, kitne words hain. Ye kaam apna apna hota hai, aapko kuch nahi karna.
Score deta hai: 100 par se kitne namber, aur kehta hai kaunsi cheez score gira rahi hai.
Pehle kaunsa kaam: Sabse zyada asar dene wale kaam ko upar rakhta hai, taaki aap pehle wahi karo.
Report + saboot banata hai: Ek file banti hai jo aap client ko bhej sakte ho, jisme likha hota hai kya toota tha, kya theek hua, aur Google ke namber kaise hilte hain.
Dobara naap kar batata hai: Agli baar jab aap jaanch chalao, wo batata hai ki score upar gaya ya neeche — taaki aap dikha sako ki kaam kar raha hai.

Jo cheezein sirf INSAN karta hai (dashboard karta nahi)

theek karna: Jo toota hai, usse site par theek karna — ye developer ya aap karte ho. Dashboard sirf batata hai kya theek karna hai.
likhna: Naya content likhna — ye writer ya aap karte ho. Dashboard brief deta hai (kya likhna hai), par article apne aap nahi likhta aur bina aapki manzoori ke site par nahi jaata.
manzoori dena: Har cheez jo site par jani hai, pehle client ki manzoori hoti hai. Ek galti jo apne aap publish ho jaye, saalon ki mehnat kha jaati hai.
Isliye aap safe hoYe dashboard aapke khilaaf nahi aapke saath hai. Wo aapke aage kaam karwa deta hai (naapna, list banana, report banana), aur jahan insaan ki soch chahiye (kaunsa kaam, kis order mein, kya theek hai) — wahan aap hoti ho. Google bhi blind automation ko saza karta hai, isliye "insaan ne dekha aur manzoor kiya" hi aapki agency ki sabse badi taakat hai.

Ye 5 kadmon ka loop hai — isko yaad rakho

  1. Naapo (automatic) — site padho, score nikalo
  2. Plan banao (automatic list, aap chunte ho) — kaunsa kaam, pehle kaunsa
  3. Approve karo (insaan) — client manzoor kare
  4. Karo (insaan/dev) — theek karo, likho, site par daalo
  5. Dobara naapo (automatic) — dikhao ki score upar gaya

Agle mahine phir wahi loop. Yahi "loop" hai jo ek baar ka kaam nahi, mahine-mahine ka paisa banata hai.

🏠 1) Overview — "aaj kya karna hai"

Ek line: Aapka roz ka dashboard. Score, aaj ka sabse top kaam, aur 4 namber — sab ek jagah. रोज़ sirf yahi tab kholo.

Yahan kya milega:

  • Health Score (0–100) + 6 chhote sub-scores (Indexability, On-page, Internal linking, Performance, Schema, International)
  • Score ke paas do chhote nishaan — data fresh (2d old) (naapne se kitne din hue) aur ▲ +1 vs pichli baar (score upar gaya ya neeche)
  • "Why this score?" button — client poochhe namber kaise aaya, to dabao
  • Aaj ki top opportunities — AI ne 4-5 sabse zyada asar dene waale kaam chun ke diye hain
  • 4 KPI cards — Organic clicks, Impressions, CTR, Average position (28 din ke)
  • Client numbers card — yahan client ke 2 namber bharte hain (neeche detail)
  • Run history card — pichhli jaanchon ka record, score kaise badha
Dekhein kya (roz ka 5 min): (1) Score aur uska trend — upar gaya ya neeche? (2) 🔴 kitni critical cheezein hain? (3) Top 3 opportunities mein se aaj kaunsa karoge?
Kya karein: Top 1 opportunity par click karo, wo aapko us tab par le jaayega jahan kaam hai. Us din sirf wohi 1-2 kaam karo, baaki kal.
Laal nishaan: 🔴 critical > 0 ho to pehle wahi theek karo — naya content likhne se pehle tooti cheezein theek honi chahiye.
Client ke 2 namber — yahi ROI banata haiOverview ke neeche card mein do khaali jagah hain: "1 lead per kitne clicks" aur "1 lead kitne ka (₹)". Client se ek baar poochho — 10 second ka sawaal. Daal diye toh har keyword ke saamne ≈leads aur ≈₹ dikhne lagenge, aur ROI tab mein teen range ban jaate hain. (Poora ROI tab section 5.10 mein hai.)

🩺 2) Website Audit — "kya toota hai, kyon, kaise theek"

Ek line: Aapki site ki poori jaanch. Har tooti cheez alag hai, kehta hai kyon mahanati hai aur theek kaise karein (code snippet ke saath).

Yahan kya milega:

  • Kitne pages padhe gaye, score kitna hai
  • Issues ki list — 🔴 critical, 🟡 improvement, ⚪ notice (filter kar sakte ho)
  • Har issue par Open dabao to milega: kyon zaroori hai, fix kya hai, code snippet, aur kaunse pages par hai
  • Sab pages ki table (title, words, status, LCP, waghera)
Dekhein kya: Sabse pehle 🔴 critical ki list — jaise noindex (page ne Google ko kaha "mat dikha"), soft 404 (page khulta hai par andar khaali), duplicate titles (2 pages ka title ek), robots blocked.
Kya karein: Red items ko "Add to approval queue" karo, phir Deliverables tab mein client ko dikhao. Sabke paas ready-made fix snippet hai — developer ko bas wo likh dena.
Laal nishaan: 🔴 wale sab din 1-7 mein theek honi chahiye. Ye theek nahi hue to upar jaane ka koi matlab nahi.

📊 3) Google Data — "asli Google ke namber"

Ek line: Jo namber Google ne aapko diye (impressions, clicks, position) — bina iske dashboard aadha hai. Ye namber site ke malik se aate hain.

Yahan kya milega:

  • 3 connection cards — Search Console, Google Analytics (GA4), WordPress
  • Organic trend (90 din) — clicks + impressions ka line chart
  • Queries table — kaunsa keyword, kitne impressions, kitne clicks, CTR, position, aur kitne CTR hone chahiye the
  • Pages table — kaunsa page kitne logon ko dikha
  • Countries / devices — log kahan se aate hain, phone se ya computer se
  • GA4 vs GSC match check — agar dono mein 20% se zyada farak hai to kahin tracking toota hai (ye ek chhota sa paisa ka nuksaan hai)
Dekhein kya: (1) Kaunse keywords zyada impressions le rahe hain par CTR kam hai — unke title theek karne se clicks badh jaate hain. (2) Kaunse position gir gaye.
Kya karein: Zyada impression + kam CTR wale keyword ka title dobara likho. Position girne par dekho competitor ne kya kiya.
Dhyan do: Ye tab tab tak khali rahega jab tak malik Search Console na de. Do raste hain: (a) malik Search Console khol ke 2 min mein "Export" dabaye, CSV de; (b) malik aapko read-only access de. Is tab mein dono ka button hai.

🔑 4) Keyword Gap — "kaam yahi se banta hai"

Ek line: Log Google par kya dhoond rahe hain, aur unmein se jin par aapki koi page hi nahi — yahi asli mauka hai. Yahan se naya kaam banta hai.

Yahan kya milega:

  • Competitor set — kaunse competitors dekhne hain (checkbox se chuno)
  • Scatter plot — x = aapki position, y = mauka (opportunity). Limed bauble = naya page banana hai.
  • Badi table — har keyword: volume, difficulty, aapki position, aapki page (ya "none"), est. traffic, ≈leads, ≈₹, score
  • Filters — Easy wins / No page yet / Priority / Low evidence
  • Topic clusters + cannibalisation (aapke apne 2 pages ek hi cheez ke liye lad rahe hain)
Dekhein kya: (1) Jin rows mein "our page = none" hai aur score high hai — wahi real content gap hai. (2) Jin par low evidence tag hai — unpar abhi bada kaam mat karo.
Kya karein: 4 aise keywords chuno (zyada dhoondhe ja rahe hain + aapki page nahi hai), unhe priority mein daalo, phir Content Plan tab mein briefs banao.
Laal nishaan: low evidence tag = 28 din mein 200 se kam baar dikhaya. "Faisla" nahi — "data pehle badhao" hai. Chhote nible par chhota kaam theek hai, naya page abhi nahi.

📈 5) Rank Tracker — "upar gayi ya neeche"

Ek line: Aapki position (jagah) kaise badli — yahi honest "kya hum behtar ho rahe hain" ka chart hai. Traffic ek noisy number hai, position zyada seedha hai.

Yahan kya milega:

  • 4 namber — kitne tracked, top 3 mein kitne, top 10 mein kitne, median kitna hil
  • Position distribution — kitne pages 1-3 par, 4-10 par, 11-20 par, 21+ par
  • Har keyword — abhi position, 7 din mein kitna hil, 28 din mein kitna hil, 30 din ka trend
Dekhein kya: (1) Distribution bae ki taraf shift ho raha hai (kam number = behtar)? (2) Kaunse bade movers hain — ▲/▼ 5 se zyada jisko.
Kya karein: Bade giravai par (▼) investigate karo — kya hua? Bade uthav par (▲) wahi karne par dobara karo jo kaam kar raha hai.

🔗 6) Backlinks — "naam kaun le raha hai"

Ek line: Kitni doosri websites aapke baare mein bolti hain (link deti hain) — aur competitor ka naam kaun leta hai aur aapka nahi.

Yahan kya milega:

  • 4 namber — backlinks, referring domains, brand anchor share, spam score
  • New & lost (4 mahine) — naye kitne aaye, kitne gaye (aapke apne check ke hisaab se)
  • Anchor mix — log aapke baare mein kya shabd likh ke link dete hain
  • Competitor link gap — kaunse domains competitor ko link dete hain par aapko nahi (yahi aapke liye naye link ke targets hain)
  • Disavow — spam links ka list (sirf draft, insaan har ek dekh kar approve kare)
Dekhein kya: Competitor link gap wala table — yahan se pata chalta hai ki kaunsi sites se aapko link maangna chahiye.
Kya karein: Ye kaam insaan karta hai — dashboard target dhoondta hai, insaan outreach kar ke link jeet-ta hai. Kisi ko kuch mat do jo usse aapke baare mein likhne ke badle na mile.
Sach batao: Bina paid index ke ye sirf dikhata hai jo aapke apne check ne dekha — history is din se hai jab aap connect kiye. Isse "poora index" ke roop mein becho nahi.

🗺️ 7) Content Plan — "kis page par kya likhna hai"

Ek line: Naya content kis order mein, kis page par, kya likhna hai — brief deta hai, article nahi. Saath mein safety guardrails jo auto-publish rokati hain.

Yahan kya milega:

  • Monthly plan — hafte mein kaunsa din kaunsa kaam. Hard cap: 4 naye pages/week (ye limit badha hi nahi ja sakti)
  • Priority queue → briefs — aapke chune keywords se briefs bante hain
  • Har brief — title options (live length counter), meta, H1, outline (H2s), entities, FAQ, internal links, schema
  • Evidence gate — kam se kam 1 apni cheez chahiye (aapka data, screenshot, quote) warna brief block rehta hai
  • Guardrails — kyun ye ek auto-publish machine nahi hai
Dekhein kya: Kaunse briefs ready hain aur kaunse mein evidence gate lag rahi hai.
Kya karein: Brief dekho → koi likhe → aap dekho → manzoor karo → "Send to WordPress as draft" dabao. Kabhi publish nahi hota, sirf draft. Evidence gate ho to pehle apni 1 cheez jodo.
Laal nishaan: 4 se zyada pages mahine mein mat banao — Google 2025-26 mein isi ko saza de raha hai. Cap isliye hai.

✅ 8) Deliverables — "kya ho gaya, manzoori baaki kya"

Ek line: Aapke kaam ka poora hisaab — kya karne ka vaada tha, kya ho gaya, aur kya abhi client ki manzoori ka intezar kar raha hai. Yahi aapki agency ka saboot hai.

Yahan kya milega:

  • Approval queue — jo cheezein site par jaani hain. Har ek par risk (low/medium/high), revertable ya nahi, aur Approve / Reject buttons
  • Diff inspector — theek se pehle vs baad, taaki aap dekh sako kya badla
  • Board — backlog → in progress → awaiting client → done (card par click karo, status badalta hai)
  • Automation rules fired — dashboard ne kaunse signals pakde (har rule → insaan ke liye task, khud kuch nahi karta)
Dekhein kya: Pending approvals — kitni cheezein client ki manzoori ka intezar kar rahi hain. Inhe aage badhna hai.
Kya karein: Low-risk items approve karo. High-risk items (noindex, redirects, disavow) kabhi bulk approve mat karo — wo hamesha insaan ek-ek karke dekhna chahiye. Ye enforced hai, preference nahi.
Yaad rakho: Har cheez jo site par jati hai, is queue se hi jati hai aur manzoori ke baad. Isliye aap kisi bhi waqt keh sakte ho — "humne bina client ki manzoori ke kuch nahi kiya."

🖨️ 9) Reports — "client ko bhejne wali report"

Ek lineMahine ke aakhir mein client ko bhejne wali report — aapki branding ke bina (white-label). Print dabao → PDF → bhejo.

Yahan kya milega:

  • Monthly report — health score, organic performance, kya humne badla (proof), priority list, agla mahina
  • CSV exports — GSC queries, audit, priority list (client spreadsheet mein dekh kar verify kar sakta hai — yahi bharosa deta hai)
  • Schedule — mahine ke 3rd tareekh par automatically
Dekhein kya: "What we changed" section bhara hua hai kya — proof ki aapne kaam kiya, sirf kahani nahi sunayi.
Kya karein: Print / Save as PDF → client ko bhejo. Iske saath ek line: "Agla mahina hum ye 4 cheezein karenge." Yahi cheez agle mahine ka paisa deta hai.

💰 10) ROI & Leads — "kaam = kitne paise"

Ek line: Is mahine ka kaam kitne leads aur kitne ₹ ka hai — range mein, promise nahi. Client jab poochhe "toh paise mein fayda kitna?", yahi tab khola karo.

Yahan kya milega:

  • Attribution rule — sirf uska natak jis program ne khud banaya (pichhle kaam ka paisa is program ka nahi hai)
  • 3 scenarios (range) — Conservative (sirf tooti cheezein theek karo), Expected (+3 aasan sudhaar), Upside (+3 naye pages). Har ek ke saath base clicks, ≈leads, ≈₹
  • Cohorts by run — pichhli jaanchon ki table, score kaise badha
  • Cost side — aapki monthly fee + cost per lead
Dekhein kya: Conservative range — yahi safe number hai jo aap client ko dikha sakte ho.
Kya karein: Pehle Overview mein client ke 2 namber (lead per clicks, lead kitne ₹) daalo. Tab yehi tab range khud bana dega. Client ko Conservative se bulavo, Expected se plan banao, Upside ko "agar sab sahi gaya" rakho.
Sach raho: Yahan "guaranteed +40% traffic" wala box hai hi nahi — jaan-boojh ke. Ye range hain, har range ke saath likha hai kaunsa maan liya. Jab aap kam waada karte ho aur zyada laate ho, tabhi agla mahine milta hai.
Score ke paas chhote nishaan — poore tab mein commonJo nishaan aap Overview mein dekhe, wo aur jagah bhi aate hain: data fresh/old (naapne se kitne din hue — 45+ par "phir se naapo"), ▲/▼ trend (pichhli jaanch se kitna badha), est. chip (jo namber aapke apne Google/crawl se nahi, third-party se hain), aur low evidence tag. Inko dekhna aapko hamesha "ye namber kitna bharosemand hai" batata hai.

7Ek mahine ka asli chakra (yahi aapki service hai)

  • Din 1 — Jaanch + list bhejo

    Us site ki poori jaanch chalao (3-4 min). Audit tab ki list ko PDF banao (Ctrl+P → Save as PDF). Client ko bhejo: "Aapki site mein 9 jagah gadbad hai, 3 aisi jinse aapke grahak aapko pa hi nahi rahe." Pehle din hi value dikhti hai — yahan se hi sale banti hai.

  • Din 2–7 — Sirf laal cheezein theek karwao

    Audit mein 🔴 wale items (duplicate titles, noindex, toote links, band pages) theek karwao. Abhi naya page mat likhwao.

  • Din 8–14 — 4 keywords, 4 brief

    Keyword Gap se 4 keywords chuno (jo zyada dhoondhe ja rahe hain aur aapki wahan page nahi hai). Har ek ke liye brief banao — kya likhna hai, kya-kya batana hai, andar kaunse link lagenge. Koi likhe, aap dekho, manzoor karo — tabhi site par jaaye.

  • Din 15–25 — Jo page 11–30 par hai, use behtar banao

    Naya page banane se ye sasta aur tez asar karta hai. Title theek karo, 2-3 naye paragraph daalo, andar se 2-3 link jodo.

  • Din 30 — Report + ROI bhejo

    ROI tab se Conservative range dekho, phir Reports tab → Print → bhejo. Isme dikhta hai ki kya kaam hua, kitne ₹ ka fayda (range), aur agla mahina kya karenge. Yahi cheez agle mahine ka paisa deta hai.

9Common sawaal

Malik Search Console nahi dega to?

Koi baat nahi. Aapka dashboard aadha chalta rahega — supply side (site ka apna haal: titles, links, pages, tooti cheezein) poori milti hai, sirf demand side (log kya dhoond rahe hain) khali rahegi. Isliye pehle 7 din ka kaam demand side ke bina bhi ho jaata hai. Baad mein wapas keh do: "Ab Google ke asli namber bhi laate hain" — aisa kehne par 9 mein se 7 maan jaate hain.

Kya ye tool site par kharabi daal sakta hai?

Nahi. Ye sirf padhta hai — jaise koi aapki dukaan ke saamne se guzra aur dekha. Wo likhta hi nahi. Aur jo likhta bhi hai (naye titles, naye paragraphs), wo client ki manzoori ke bina site par nahi jaata.

Main terminal nahi jaanta to?

Terminal sirf 2 jagah chahiye: (1) pehli baar mein node tools/go.js ek line — phir browser mein sab box se hota hai. (2) Agar aap khud report banane ka order dena chahein: 3 line ki list (yeh booklet ke aakhir mein hai). Baaki sab browser mein hai.

Mobile se chala sakta hoon?

Dashboard desktop ke liye banaya hai. Mobile par tab badalna mushkil ho sakta hai. Agar aap ko mobile se chahiye toh ek baar batayein — main bana dunga.

Kitni sites ek saath?

Itni jitni chahiye. Har site ki apni report file hoti hai, apna apna naam. Aap ek laptop par 10 clients ka dashboard ek saath chala sakte ho.

Kya main Google ke rulaoon ke khilaaf kar raha hoon?

Nahi. Ye tool sirf padhta hai, aur ek baar har 250 millisecond mein — jaise koi aam insaan padhta hai. Wo kisi ka account nahi todta, koi paid service nahi chhupta, aur koi data aap ke aage nahi bechta.

Client poochhe: "toh paise mein fayda kitna?"

Rojagari ke 2 namber poochho — "1 lead kitne organic clicks mein se banti hai, aur 1 lead aapko kitna kamata hai?" — Overview ke client card mein daal do. Tab ROI tab khud teen range bana dega: Conservative / Expected / Upside. Client ko Conservative range se hi bulawa do — jab aap kam waada karte ho aur usse zyada laate ho, tabhi agle mahine ka paisa milta hai.

Score badhega ya nahi? Kitne mahine mein?

Sach bolo: range de sakte ho, vaada nahi. Pehla mahina aksar sirf tooti cheezein theek hota hai — namber tabhi hilta hai jab wo theek kiya hua Google ke paas pahunchta hai (do hafele se do mahine). Har mahine ke re-run se ROI tab ki "Cohorts by run" table mein ek point badhta hai — wohi table dikhata hai ki naya content apni age ke saath kya kar raha hai. Month-1 near-zero normal hai, aur is dashboard mein wo cheez chhupane ka button hi nahi hai — yahi aapki imandari ka nishaan hai.

File mein history kyun jama hoti jaati hai?

Har baar jab aap jaanch chalaate ho ya wahi file wapis kholte ho, ek chhota sa point save hota hai — date, score, pages, clicks. Point wahi file ke andar jama hote hain, toh jo file aap client ko bhejte ho, woh khud yaad rakhti hai ki pichhle mahine kya tha. Kisi doosri jagah kuch save nahi hota, koi cloud nahi, koi account nahi.
